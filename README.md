# E-Commerce Business Performance Analytics

## Project Overview

This project analyzes end-to-end e-commerce business performance using Python, SQL, and Power BI. The objective is to identify revenue trends, customer purchasing behavior, product performance, and operational insights to support data-driven business decisions.

The project follows a complete analytics workflow including data cleaning, validation, SQL analysis, and interactive dashboard development.

---

- Python (Pandas, NumPy)
- MySQL
- Power BI
- Microsoft Excel

## Dataset

The dataset consists of approximately 80,000+ customer events and multiple relational tables:

- Users
- Products
- Orders
- Order Items
- Reviews
- Events

---

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Cleaning & Validation (Python)

- Imported and validated multiple datasets
- Converted date columns into datetime format
- Checked for missing values
- Removed invalid records
- Validated quantity, price, item totals, and ratings
- Performed Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Generated business KPIs

---

### 2. Business Analysis (SQL)

Performed SQL analysis using joins, aggregations, subqueries, and grouping to answer business questions such as:

- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Total Products Sold
- Monthly Revenue Trend
- Highest Revenue Month
- Top Revenue Categories
- Top Revenue Products
- Top Spending Customers
- Orders by Status
- Returned Orders by Category
- Brand Performance
- Category Performance
- Customer Ratings
- Revenue by City
- Revenue by Weekday
- Monthly New User Signups
- Customer Journey Funnel (View → Cart → Wishlist → Purchase)

---

### 3. Dashboard Development (Power BI)

## Key Business Insights

- Generated total business revenue of **11.92M**.
- Processed **20,000** customer orders.
- Sold over **60,000** products.
- Identified highest revenue-generating brands and categories.
- Analyzed top-spending customers.
- Tracked customer satisfaction using product and category ratings.
- Identified product categories with high return rates.
- Visualized customer conversion from product views to purchases.
- Monitored monthly customer acquisition and operational performance.
